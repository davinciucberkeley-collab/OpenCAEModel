K09-L Revision B

Five design changes take the K09-L from three failing requirements to none. Every requirement is now met or predicted to be met, and the transition holds altitude in the 6-DOF model. The aircraft is ready to build and test. It is not approved to fly: the predictions rest on estimated propeller, aerodynamic and material data that only the bench and flight tests can replace.

02The five changes

Each change is a selectable option in the platform (Configuration page: landing feet_b, mount inboard_b; parameters throttle_max, transition_*, shipping), applied to a copy of the imported CAD. Revision A stays intact and you can switch back to it at any time.

B1

Outrigger feet (K09L-AF-FOOT-B)

addresses R6
What
Foot A stretched parametrically beyond its fin slot: outboard edge |z| 198 -> 220 mm (a solid 30 mm outrigger beyond the fin tip), aft wall 6 mm thinner (ground plane x 320 -> 314 mm), side walls 9 -> 5 mm with the clamp screw M3x30 -> M3x25. The slot and its fit on the fin are unchanged.
Why
Revision A tips over at 34.7 deg, below the 36 deg lean the wind study gives for the 8 m/s flight-card gust. The governing support-polygon edge is the feet's outer edge; widening it and lowering the CG above the ground plane raises the tip-over angle. Thinner walls keep the feet mass-neutral, because mass at the feet (240 mm aft of the CG) costs static margin.
Evidence
Geometry: CAD of foot A, modified parametrically (no new CAD exists yet). Mass: CAD mass x volume ratio (ESTIMATE). Root bending check uses an estimated PETG strength.
B2

Motor sets 6 mm inboard (motor tube 368 mm, K09L-AF-FIN-B)

addresses R5
What
The CF motor tube is cut 368 mm long instead of 380 mm (same 24 in stock) and each motor set (elbow unchanged, motor, prop hardware, mount screws) sits 6 mm further inboard: motor axis |z| 206 -> 200 mm. The fin's leading-edge notch that receives the elbow is cut 6 mm deeper so the elbow keeps its clearance.
Why
Revision A is 454 mm high (motor mounts at |z| 227 mm), 4 mm over the 450 mm side of the transport box. Revision B is 442 mm, an 8 mm margin. The elbow sits flush on the fin tip, so the fin notch has to follow; the assembly check confirms no new interference.
Evidence
Geometry: rigid translation of the CAD bodies and the prop joints, shortened tube and deeper notch (ANALYTICAL). Tube and fin masses scale with their volume (ESTIMATE).
B3

Motor output limit 0.66 in the flight controller

addresses R7
What
Full stick commands 66% of the battery voltage to the motors (e.g. ArduPilot Q_M_SPIN_MAX / a motor output limit; set and verify on the thrust stand).
Why
At full throttle on a full pack the X3108S would draw about 38.5 A against its 25 A (30 s) rating. The aircraft has thrust to spare (T/W 4.3), so a limit keeps the CAD-verified motor, adds no mass and keeps T/W >= 1.6 at the end of the battery. 0.66 is the highest limit that keeps a margin (1.07) on the rating; above 0.70 the full-throttle current exceeds it. The alternative, the X2814 V3 (50 A), has no CAD or published bolt pattern, adds 108 g, and its transition failed in the model.
Evidence
Operating points use the supplier motor data and the ESTIMATED prop coefficients: CONDITIONAL until the thrust-stand test (T-PROP-01) measures the current at the limit.
B5

Transition procedure: 80 deg pitch-over at 60 deg/s, 16 m/s

addresses G3
What
Forward transition pitches the nose over to 80 deg from vertical at 60 deg/s and declares wing-borne flight at 16 m/s (was 75 deg, 45 deg/s, 14 m/s); the back-transition enters from 16 m/s.
Why
With the motor output limited (B3) the upper motors saturate during a 75 deg pitch-over and the aircraft loses about 23 m. Pitching further over puts the wing to work sooner; 16 m/s keeps the wing away from the stall. Swept in the 6-DOF model: 70 deg loses 22-25 m at any rate, 80 deg holds altitude, and 12 m/s loses about 24 m even without any output limit.
Evidence
REDUCED-ORDER simulation on estimated aerodynamic and prop coefficients; verify in a high-altitude transition test before relying on it.
B4

Shipping as a labelled provisional estimate

addresses R3
What
Shipping = number of separate orders x $14 (ESTIMATE, +/- 50 %).
Why
Revision A has no shipping figure, so the reserve cannot be evaluated (a missing cost is never counted as zero). The reserve stays above $309 even at +50 % on this estimate.
Evidence
ESTIMATE: replace with the real order totals.

Assembly check

1 interference in Rev B: the same 0.1 mm wing-core / pod-shell contact the imported CAD already has (foam compliance). The outrigger feet, the shorter motor tube and the deeper fin notch add none; an automated test keeps it that way. 40 expected contacts are confirmed. 2 harness ends are left for review: receiver CRSF and servo extension, unchanged from A. The motor bolt pattern is still unverified (SunnySky drawing not available).

Hover simulation

6-DOF hover, 10 s at 5 m: holds position within 0.9 mm. Hover current and power are practically unchanged (the output limit is far above the 42 % hover throttle).

Forward transition

The output limit that protects the motors also takes away the thrust reserve the transition was using. With A's procedure (pitch over to 75° at 45°/s, wing-borne at 14 m/s), B lost 23 m of height. Procedure B pitches further over (80°, at 60°/s) and accelerates to 16 m/s, so the wing carries the aircraft sooner. In calm air Rev B now loses 0.37 m, against 0.19 m for Rev A, and A gets there by driving its motors to 40 A.

The 29 A peaks in Rev B are rotor-acceleration transients lasting about 0.2 s. Steady full-stick current is 23.3 A. Rev A spends seconds above the rating in most off-nominal cases (table below).

What this means

Rev B trades gust tolerance at part charge for staying inside the motor rating. In calm air it transitions down to 30 % charge. In a 4 m/s side gust it needs a fresh pack. A 4 m/s tailwind gust during the pitch-over defeats both revisions. These become flight-card limits (section 06). They are model results on provisional aerodynamics and need the transition test to confirm them.

06Flight-card limits for Rev B

  • Forward transition in calm air (gusts up to 2 m/s) only with at least 30 % battery charge: calm-air transitions lose 0.7-1.8 m at 30-60 % charge and end in ground contact at 20 % in the model.
  • Forward transition in side or head gusts up to 4 m/s only with at least 90 % charge (fresh pack): a 4 m/s side gust ends in ground contact at 60-80 % charge and loses 0.5 m at 90 %. This is the price of the 0.66 output limit; a thrust-stand measurement of the real prop may allow a higher limit.
  • No forward transition in tailwind gusts above 2 m/s: a 4 m/s tailwind gust during the pitch-over ends in ground contact in the model (revision A already fails at 2 m/s).
  • Transition at a safe height (30 m in the simulations) until test flights confirm the model.

Set the motor output limit to 0.66 in the flight controller (for ArduPilot quadplane tailsitters, the Q_M_ motor output range; confirm the parameter for the firmware in use) and verify it on the thrust stand (T04) before the first flight.

Open inputs that limit the evidence

  • B-rep CAD — STEP / .f3d files are on the team PC (C:\Users\trist\K09L_build); only the tessellated Fusion export is available here. FEA geometry is rebuilt from part dimensions.
  • Motor bolt pattern — Modelled 4 x M3 on 19 x 25 mm; SunnySky drawing not reached. Measure.
  • FC and servo mounting — FC 30.5 x 26.5 from a manual sketch (one vendor lists 30 x 30).
  • Propeller performance — APC PER3 file for 11x4.5MR not reachable: C_T, C_P are provisional.
  • Battery internal resistance — Not published; provisional 4 mOhm/cell.
  • Aerodynamic coefficients — No wind-tunnel or CFD data for K09-L: C_D0, e, neutral point provisional.
  • Material strengths (PETG, XPS, Depron) — Printed-part and foam strengths not yet tested.
  • Shipping — Not in the K09-L cost files.
  • Revision-B parts — foot B, the 368 mm motor tube and the fin notch exist as parametric changes of the imported CAD; model them in Fusion before printing and cutting.
  • Motor supply — no US stock of the X3108S KV900 was found when the catalog was built (robu.in lists it); check lead time.
